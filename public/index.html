The procurement problem

Teams usually look for a backup supplier after the current one becomes a problem.

Then someone spends days finding vendors, validating technical fit, chasing quotes, normalizing terms, and negotiating.

Vendor Scout keeps that procurement loop moving before the supply chain breaks.

The agent does the repetitive work. The human keeps control of the commitment.
