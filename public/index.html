Find better suppliers. Negotiate automatically. Approve the best deal.

Vendor Scout is an autonomous procurement agent for hardware teams. It searches the supplier market, qualifies what it finds, requests real quotes, negotiates the terms, and stops at the human decision.

Live sourcing mission

One hardware need becomes a continuous sourcing mission.

The agent owns the repetitive procurement work end to end. Each stage persists its evidence, so every number on screen can be traced back to where it came from.

The agent earns the decision. It never makes it.

Vendor Scout can research, contact, and negotiate. It stops before money moves or terms are accepted, and hands over a decision packet with the economics, the competing offers, and the evidence behind both.

Built on a persistent agent session with a hard approval boundary.

01

12-tool MCP boundary

Discovery, qualification, outreach, negotiation, and comparison are exposed as explicit procurement tools rather than free-form actions.

02

Sandboxed quote analysis

Quotes are normalized to one currency and compared on landed cost. Unknown costs stay unknown instead of making an offer look cheaper.

03

Destructive tools pause

The sample-order tool is marked destructive and pauses for approval immediately before execution, after the business decision is already recorded.

Procurement keeps moving. Humans keep control.
